## 2. The strategic crisis

_The big-picture business risk. If the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?_

> If we do nothing for 12 months, the company will risk churn to leaner competitors that have not inherited the historical burden of RouteLogic. RouteLogic continues to be positioned as an administrative hassle, and customers may choose to build their own point solutions with the reduced overhead of build vs. buy.

## 3. The moment of misery

_The specific point where the product fails the user, forcing a manual workaround (Google, spreadsheets, group texts, a competitor)._

> The user is currently forced to export their data and work outside the platform to avoid the extra layers of functionality that aren't relevant to them.

## 4. Problem hook

_One urgent sentence fusing the business risk and the user pain, your pitch for why this is the most important thing to work on right now._

> We must solve the risk of customer churn by addressing the administrative burden of using RouteLogic, so customers stop seeking alternate execution paths due to friction in completing core tasks.

## 5. Value proposition

_Who it's for, the new value, and why it's urgent to launch now._

> For **enterprise logistics customers**, we will **consolidate and simplify configuration and administration of core workflows into a single suite that cuts the time to complete 80% of actions, while exposing advanced functionality selectively to persona-based user roles** because **we need to retain high-value enterprise customers before they move to leaner alternatives**.
